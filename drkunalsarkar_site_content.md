### Doctor Profile
- **Name:** Dr. Kunal Sarkar
- **Credentials:** MBBS, DNB, FRCS(Ed), FRCS(Glasg)
- **Position:** Chief Cardiac Surgeon and Director, Manipal Hospital, EM Bypass, Mukundapur, Kolkata
- **Profile image:** `/admin/blocks/pages/images/drks.png`

### Biography (Full Text)
Dr. Kunal Sarkar is a distinguished cardiac surgeon with over three decades of experience, leading authority in complex cardiovascular procedures and a fervent advocate for innovative surgical techniques. He heads the cardiac department at Manipal Hospital, EM Bypass, Mukundapur, Kolkata.

His expertise spans: off-pump CABG, bloodless heart surgery, total arterial bypass grafting, combined surgeries (CABG + Valve Surgery), and complex aortic surgeries. He is especially known for performing more than 90% of coronary surgeries without a heart-lung machine and for complex redo surgeries.

**Academic background:** Gold medalist in surgery from Medical College, Calcutta (12 other awards). National Scholar. Trained in the UK at Cardiothoracic Centre Liverpool and St. Mary's Hospital. Fellow of the Royal College of Surgeons in both Edinburgh and Glasgow. Faculty member at Imperial College London.

**Associations:** Intimately involved with EACTS and IACTS (past President). Active member of the South Asian Forum of Cardiothoracic Surgeons.

**Beyond surgery:** President of Calcutta Debating Circle (CDC); organises Calcutta's Festival of the Spoken Word — India's largest live debate event. President of Kolkata Sukriti Foundation. Chairman of Kolkata Heart Foundation. Managing Partner of Asklepia Health LLP (Eastern India's fastest-growing digital health platform).
